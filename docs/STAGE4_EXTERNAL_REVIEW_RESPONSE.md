# Stage 4 response to the 9 August 2026 external review

## Purpose

This is an internal revision crosswalk for the next independent adequacy review. It is not a
journal response letter. The revision follows the approved bounded route: repair conclusions that
depend on directional model space and empirical membership, then frame the paper as an
exchangeable-date retention experiment. It does not add site histories, weaker signals or a new
all-rule realised-noise run.

## Major issues

## Claims that should be challenged in the next adequacy review

1. Whether the bounded exchangeable-date result is sufficiently archaeologically consequential for
   the *Journal of Archaeological Science* without a site-history generator.
2. Whether the empirical benchmark and nine sensitivities justify retaining bracketed retention in
   the main ordering claim, given that only oldest and youngest received the full sensitivity gate.
3. Whether the untested realised-noise behaviour of one-sided retention requires further analysis
   before submission or is adequately disclosed as residual scope.
4. Whether the ten-fold design is now qualified often enough that readers cannot mistake random
   multi-date performance for a general field recommendation.
5. Whether the repository and data-governance plan is adequate once the actual replacement archive
   is assembled.
