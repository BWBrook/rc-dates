# Highlights

- Keeping each site's oldest or youngest date manufactured apparent decline or growth.
- Selecting dates by age reversed opposing simulated regional trends.
- With 75% of sites treated, weak simulated trends were reversed in 93–100% of runs.
- Keeping both extrema at all sites made ten-fold linear trends appear exponential.
- Oldest-date retention shifted the AustArch curve mean over 3,100 years older.
