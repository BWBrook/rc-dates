![Baseline aggregate smoothed curves for the AustArch reference dataset. Plotted are the summed probability distribution (SPD; sum of calibrated posteriors followed by a 100-year running mean), 100-year binned frequency distribution (FD), and Gaussian kernel density estimate (KDE; bandwidth = 100 yr) centred on each date's median age, calibrated with SHCal20 or Marine20 as appropriate. Each curve is normalised to unit area; for joint display the SPD and KDE were re-binned to the FD 100-year grid. This is the full-dataset baseline against which Figure 2's sub-sampling scenarios (50% and 100% of sites treated by various within-site rules) are compared. Displays here are descriptive only; inferential results use likelihoods built directly from calibrated posteriors rather than any single smoothing device. Axes: years cal BP (12 ka to present) and scaled density (×10⁻⁴).](../figures/fig_1.png){#fig-main-1 width=95%}

![Consequences of within-site sub-sampling on the AustArch reference dataset. Paired panels show five strategies, oldest, youngest, bracketed (oldest + youngest), random-1 (one random date per treated site), and random-5. Each strategy is shown with mean ± 95% Monte Carlo ribbon at two sampling intensities: 50% of sites treated by the rule (remainder contribute all dates) and 100% treated. Curves plot SPD, KDE, and FD summaries normalised to unit area; SPD/KDE were re-binned to the FD 100-year grid for joint display. All determinations were calibrated with SHCal20/Marine20. A single Monte Carlo realisation comprises: i) drawing the treated 50% of sites (if applicable); ii) selecting date indices per rule; iii) appending all dates from untreated sites; and iv) computing the three aggregators. Stochastic rules (random-1, random-5) use 1,000 realisations; deterministic rules (oldest, youngest, bracketed) vary only by the site subset (1,000 draws). The 0% baseline (“all dates”) is shown in Figure 1. These displays show how common field constraints reshape regional aggregates independently of smoother choice; inferential results use posterior-based likelihoods rather than the smoothed curves themselves. Axes: years cal BP (12 ka to present) and scaled value.](../figures/fig_2.png){#fig-main-2 width=95%}
