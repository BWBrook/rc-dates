![Change in empirical calendar-age density after post hoc within-site retention. Rows show oldest, youngest, bracketed (oldest plus youngest), random-1 and random-5 retention; columns show treatment of 50% or 100% of the 68 sites. Each curve is the difference between a retained unit-integral summary and that method's own all-date baseline, so positive values mark excess relative density and zero marks no change. SPD, FD and KDE use identical selected dates and a common vertical scale. Lines are means; shading spans pointwise 2.5th–97.5th percentiles across 1,000 conditional realisations. Full-site boundary scenarios are deterministic and have no envelope. The envelopes describe conditional retention realisations, not uncertainty over the archive or regional population history. ![Truth-consistent signed-state selection under post hoc date retention. Rows are the five generating regional event-age densities; columns are retention rules; panels give the site-treatment proportion.
