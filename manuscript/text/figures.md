![Baseline aggregate curves for the AustArch reference set. Plotted are the summed probability distribution (SPD; posterior mass from all 976 overlap-eligible dates followed by a 100-year running mean), 100-year binned frequency distribution (FD; displayed with a two-bin running mean), and Gaussian kernel density estimate (KDE; bandwidth = 100 yr) centred on the 889 weighted-mean calibrated ages inside the analysis window. The FD also uses these 889 point summaries. Each curve is normalised to unit area; SPD and KDE are shown on the FD's 100-year grid. The display uses a padded 12,000-0 cal BP plotting range, while the analysis window is 11,650-651 cal BP. These method-specific displays are descriptive only; inference uses likelihoods built directly from calibrated posteriors. Axes: years cal BP and scaled density ($\times 10^{-4}$).](../figures/fig_1.png){#fig-main-1 width=95%}

![Within-site sub-sampling in the AustArch reference set. Rows: oldest, youngest, bracketed (oldest + youngest), random-1, and random-5. Left panels apply each rule to half the sites, with other sites retaining all dates; right panels apply it to all sites. Curves are unit-area SPD, KDE, and two-bin-smoothed FD summaries on a common 100-year grid and scale. Shading gives pointwise 2.5th-97.5th percentile conditional-realisation envelopes; full-site oldest, youngest, and bracketed rules are deterministic. Boundary-specific effective counts accompany the reproducibility output. Figure 1 gives the all-dates reference. Panels are descriptive; inference fits calibrated posteriors. Axes: padded 12,000-0 cal BP and scaled density ($\times 10^{-4}$).](../figures/fig_2.png){#fig-main-2 width=57%}

![Accuracy of within-site sampling strategies versus the fraction of sites subjected to the rule. Accuracy is the share of simulation replicates in which AIC selects the generating growth family (uniform, linear, or exponential); the dotted line marks chance accuracy (1/3). Each point pools 1,000 replicates from each family ($n = 3{,}000$); vertical bars are paired cluster-bootstrap 95% intervals from 1,000 resamples of replicate identifiers, retaining the three generating-family outcomes together. The all-dates reference has no sub-sampling intensity and is repeated across the x-axis for comparison. These curve-expectation production results use the $\ell^1$ likelihood; $\ell^2$ and paired realised-assay checks are reported in Supplementary Information. Axes: percentage of sites sub-sampled (50-100%) and accuracy.](../figures/fig_3.pdf){#fig-main-3 width=95%}

![Parameter bias induced by within-site sampling rules under exponential (left) and linear (right) growth models. Points show mean bias in the recovered parameter; horizontal bars span the 2.5th to 97.5th percentiles of replicate-level bias at each sampling intensity (50%, 75%, 100%). Scales differ between panels to reveal structure; the exponential-rate panel is on the order of $10^{-4}$ and the linear-slope panel on the order of $10^{-8}$. Zero bias is indicated by the dashed line. Youngest-date sampling preferentially selects later contexts and therefore produces the sign-opposed positive exponential-rate bias visible in the left panel. These curve-expectation production results use the $\ell^1$ likelihood; $\ell^2$ and paired realised-assay checks are reported in Supplementary Information.](../figures/fig_4.pdf){#fig-main-4 width=95%}
